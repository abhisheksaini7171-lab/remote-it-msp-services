Partnership operating model

Clear responsibilities before the first ticket.

We work to an agreed operating model rather than assuming access, coverage or service levels.

1. Discover

Discuss your service desk, tool stack, customer environments, ticket types, volumes, time zones and desired outcomes.

2. Define

Agree scope, named contacts, access permissions, escalation paths, communications, quality expectations and coverage windows in writing.

3. Onboard

Review documentation, runbooks, ticket templates, approval requirements and customer-specific procedures before work is assigned.

4. Deliver and review

Work through approved channels, update tickets, document changes and use regular reviews to refine the arrangement.

Security and accountability

Operate within your controls.

  • Customer-approved access and least-privilege permissions
  • Named accounts and MFA where supported by your environment
  • Change approvals and escalation contacts agreed before work
  • Ticket notes, activity records and structured handovers
  • Confidentiality and customer-specific procedures documented
Flexible engagement

Start with a defined scope.

  • Ad-hoc or hourly engineering tasks
  • Project-based delivery
  • Agreed recurring ticket or support capacity
  • Extended or after-hours coverage by prior agreement
  • Dedicated arrangements subject to capacity and written scope

Rates, availability, response targets and service levels are confirmed individually after discovery. No universal 24/7 SLA is implied.
