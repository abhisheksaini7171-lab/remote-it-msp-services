Common questions

What to expect before getting started.

Do you support customers outside India?

Yes. The service is designed for worldwide remote delivery. Time zones, coverage windows and service responsibilities are agreed for each engagement.

Can you work as a white-label partner for an MSP?

Yes. We can discuss agreed ticket queues, escalation processes, documentation standards, customer communication rules and project support behind your brand.

Do you offer 24/7 support?

24/7 engineering coverage can be discussed by team arrangement and is subject to the agreed scope, roster and service-level terms. It is not an automatic or unconditional coverage promise.

Do you publish fixed prices?

Pricing depends on the support scope, skills required, coverage window and engagement model. We discuss the requirement and provide a proposal before work begins.

How do we begin?

Send an enquiry with a high-level description of your environment and needs. The team aims to respond to new enquiries within 24 hours to discuss next steps.
