Privacy notice

How we handle enquiry information.

This notice explains the information collected through this website's contact form and how it is used.

Information collected

When you submit an enquiry, we may receive your name, business email, company, country, time zone, customer type, service interests, engagement preference and message.

Purpose and use

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, assess service fit, communicate about a potential engagement and maintain related business correspondence. Do not submit passwords, API keys, access tokens, customer records or infrastructure secrets through this website.

Form processing

The contact form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Information submitted is transmitted to that provider and delivered to the business email address shown on the contact page. Review the provider's privacy terms before submitting information.

Retention and requests

Enquiry information is retained only as reasonably needed for business communication, recordkeeping and legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion, contact abhisheksaini7171@gmail.com.

Security and updates

We take reasonable steps to limit access to enquiry information. Internet transmission is not guaranteed to be completely secure. This notice may be updated as the website and services evolve.

This is a starter privacy notice and should be reviewed for the jurisdictions where the business operates before launch.
