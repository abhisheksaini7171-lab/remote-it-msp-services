Privacy notice

How we handle enquiry information.

This notice describes the information you may provide through this website and how it is used to respond to business enquiries.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who is responsible

Remote IT & MSP Services is responsible for handling enquiries received through this website. For privacy questions or requests, contact abhisheksaini7171@gmail.com.

Information you may provide

The contact form may collect your name, business email address, company or MSP name, country, time zone, customer type, approximate user or device count, preferred support coverage, engagement preference, priority, selected services and the details you write in your message.

Why we use it

We use this information to review and respond to your enquiry, understand the requested services and operating hours, assess whether the requested engagement may be suitable, arrange follow-up discussions and maintain related business correspondence.

Form delivery provider and acknowledgement emails

The contact form uses FormSubmit to transmit submitted information and deliver it to our business email inbox. FormSubmit also sends an automatic acknowledgement to the email address entered in the form. This means the information submitted, including the contact email address, is processed by that third-party service to deliver the enquiry and acknowledgement. Please review FormSubmit's own privacy information before submitting. We do not control the provider's independent systems or retention practices.

Retention and disclosure

We keep enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, maintain relevant business records and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not ask you to submit credentials, passwords, API keys, access tokens, customer personal records or confidential infrastructure details through this form. We do not sell enquiry information. Information may be disclosed where necessary to operate the enquiry process or where required by law.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of enquiry information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Email abhisheksaini7171@gmail.com and describe your request. Do not include passwords or sensitive information in the request.

Cookies and analytics

This website is a static GitHub Pages site. We have not intentionally added advertising or analytics tracking to the website. Hosting and third-party form providers may process technical information as described in their own notices.

Security and international enquiries

We take reasonable steps to limit access to enquiry information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Because we accept worldwide enquiries and use a third-party form delivery provider, information may be processed in locations outside your country.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website, enquiry process or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

This is a practical starter notice, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Before actively marketing in multiple countries, have it reviewed for the privacy laws applicable to the business and the locations of your customers.
